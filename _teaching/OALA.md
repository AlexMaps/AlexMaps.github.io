Designed and delivered an Intro to GIS course to build technical geospatial capacity among First Nation Land Staff across Ontario. Recognizing varying community resources and software access, the complete curriculum was taught in both ArcGIS and QGIS. The course focused on practical, real-world FN lands workflows including creating site sketches from cadastral data, automating site sketches using Map Series, creating land use planning maps, land cover change analysis, basic spatial analysis and setting up FieldMaps/QField for data collection in the field.
